This tool helps your team identify strengths and areas for improvement. You receive real-time feedback and detailed reports to help your group work better together.

📊 About the Tool

Teams need clear data to improve. This diagnostic tool provides a simple way to measure how prepared your group is for modern workflows. You answer a series of questions, and the platform generates a final report to guide your next steps.

💻 System Requirements

Your computer needs the following to run this software:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • 4GB of RAM
  • At least 200MB of free disk space
  • An active internet connection for real-time reporting

📥 How to Install

Follow these steps to set up the software on your Windows computer:

  1. Go to the official download page.
  2. Click on the latest release file to save the installer to your computer.
  3. Open the file once the download finishes.
  4. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the installation.
  5. Open the application from your desktop shortcut once the process finishes.

⚙️ Getting Started

When you open the tool for the first time, it asks you to input your team details. You then select the assessment module that fits your current project phase. Answer the questions honestly to receive the most accurate feedback.

📈 Viewing Results

The dashboard displays your final scores as soon as you submit your answers. You see charts and text summaries that highlight your team's readiness. You can save these reports as PDF files to share with your colleagues.

🛠 Troubleshooting

If the program fails to start, restart your computer and try again. Ensure that your security software permits the application to access the internet. If problems remain, visit the repository page and check the issues tab for help.
